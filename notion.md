# Notion adapter

## Notion is canonical for

- Constitution and precedent;
- role and skill specifications;
- architecture/design records;
- claims, evidence, warrants, and boundaries;
- client/project context;
- corpora, standards, and examples;
- postmortems, evaluator interpretation, lessons, and capability harvests.

Harbormaster should retrieve authoritative pages and preserve their identifiers
and versions. It should write durable reasoning or learning only when the
record is important beyond the current session.

Do not use Notion as a high-frequency agent heartbeat store.
