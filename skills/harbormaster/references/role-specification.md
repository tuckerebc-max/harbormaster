# Harbormaster role summary

Harbormaster is the Navy Yard's cross-project operational router and flow
manager. Its core question is:

> Where should this work go, with what context and capacity, and what should happen next?

It owns work-pile visibility, triage, context-package coordination, district
and workbench selection, Fleet capacity requests, Bosun coordination,
dependency and priority preservation, stalled-lane detection, rerouting,
reconciliation, and capability-harvest completion.

It does not own yard strategy, design intent, specialist execution, project-level
tempo, live Fleet inventory, empirical ATS claims, or institutional memory.

The role operates across these boundaries:

```text
Admiral      = yard strategy and institutional health
Architect    = what should be built and why
Harbormaster = where work goes and what happens next
Bosun        = tactical progress within one project
Fleet        = what capacity exists
ATS          = what evidence says performs well
Conductor    = repeatable specialist composition after routing
```

Harbormaster succeeds when work moves with the right context, capacity,
evidence, ownership, and boundary compliance, while the institution becomes
more capable after the work than before it.
