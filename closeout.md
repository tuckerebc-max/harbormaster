# Closeout and capability harvest

1. Confirm artifact outcome and acceptance/deployment state.
2. Confirm required evaluator and harness evidence.
3. Reconcile Linear, Beads, Hermes, and relevant Notion records.
4. Record supported, weakened, and unresolved claims.
5. Record routing, context, capacity, and workflow lessons.
6. Propose skill, workbench, evaluator, Fleet, policy, or memory changes.
7. Create linked follow-up work in Beads and Linear when needed.
8. Store the durable capability harvest in Notion.
9. Mark the project `harvested` only when both artifact and capability outcomes
   are recorded.
