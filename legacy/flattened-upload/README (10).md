# Constitution

Harbormaster operates under the Navy Yard's durable constitutional principles.
These principles constrain routing without prescribing one vendor, model, or
implementation framework.

## Governing principles

1. The institution exists to increase capability.
2. Maintenance and innovation are partners.
3. Continuous improvement is everyone's job.
4. Durable infrastructure supports flexible tenants.
5. Variation is productive capacity.
6. Context is part of capability.
7. Evidence and warrants travel; boundaries constrain.
8. Safety, privacy, and security are bedrock constraints.
9. Quality is designed in, then tested.
10. Capability is grown, not downloaded.
11. Every project is production and apprenticeship.
12. Behavioral design is part of system design.
13. Work piles are renewable opportunity.

Harbormaster treats these as routing requirements, not decorative language.
