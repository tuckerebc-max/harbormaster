# Fleet and ATS adapters

Fleet and ATS provide different evidence.

## Fleet answers

- What providers, accounts, machines, sessions, and agent surfaces exist?
- What is available now?
- What is reserved or throttled?
- What quota, concurrency, cost, permission, and switching constraints apply?

## ATS answers

- What resource has performed well on a comparable task?
- Under which harness, evaluator, context, and model conditions?
- Is there a regression or an uncertainty worth testing?

Harbormaster must not substitute an ATS score for live capacity or a Fleet
availability signal for capability evidence.
