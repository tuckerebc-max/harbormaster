# Federated Work Graph

The graph is not necessarily a new graph database. It is the typed relationship
among existing records.

```text
Linear commitment
       |
       v
Beads executable work ──> Superpowers execution evidence
       |
       v
Hermes routing/reconciliation ──> Fleet capacity + ATS capability evidence
       |
       v
Notion design, precedent, memory, and capability harvest
       |
       v
Navy Yard Chart projection
```

## Essential relationships

```text
project contains work_item
work_item depends_on work_item
work_item governed_by design_record
design_record asserts claim
claim supported_by evidence
evidence connected_by warrant
decision constrained_by boundary
work_item routed_to workbench
work_item assigned_to bosun
work_item consumes fleet_resource
evaluation_run measures capability
project produces capability_harvest
```

The relationship itself often matters as much as the record. Preserve relation
type, source, authority, provenance, and version.
