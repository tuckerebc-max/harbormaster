# Superpowers adapter

Superpowers is the execution method after Harbormaster routes work.

The handoff should supply the context package, design record, implementation
boundary, acceptance criteria, worktree/write scope, evaluator expectations,
and Bosun communication path. Superpowers then governs planning,
implementation, review, testing, and verification.

Harbormaster observes the resulting evidence and route state. It does not
replace Superpowers' specialist execution method.
