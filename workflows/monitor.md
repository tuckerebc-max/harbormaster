# Monitor cross-project flow

## Observe

Read events and records for:

- new or changed work;
- ready-frontier changes;
- Bosun progress evidence;
- dependency resolution or deadlock;
- Fleet availability, throttling, and reservation expiry;
- evaluator failures and regressions;
- context or policy conflicts;
- deployment and closeout transitions.

## Decide

For each material exception, determine whether it is waiting, blocked,
stalled, failing, festering, or lost. Select the smallest justified response
from the [recovery ladder](../skills/harbormaster/references/monitoring-recovery.md).

## Communicate

Record only decision-relevant updates. Linear gets meaningful project state;
Notion gets durable reasoning and learning; Beads gets executable state;
Hermes retains the routing/reconciliation event; the Chart gets a projection.
